Who We Are

Support that meets you
where you are.

Clarity On the Horizon is a professional Employee Assistance Program dedicated to supporting the mental, emotional, and practical wellbeing of individuals and their families. We partner with employers to bring compassionate, confidential care directly to the workplace.

🌅

Hope-Centered

We believe every person has the capacity to heal, grow, and find clarity — no matter where they are in their journey.

🔒

Strictly Confidential

Your privacy is our highest priority. Nothing you share leaves our care without your express consent.

🤝

Accessible & Inclusive

We meet you where you are — in person, by phone, or virtually — with no barriers to entry and no judgment.

What We Offer

Comprehensive care
for every need.

From mental health counseling to financial coaching, our licensed professionals provide the full spectrum of support your employees need to thrive — at work and at home.

🧠

Mental Health & Wellness

Support for anxiety, depression, burnout, trauma, and emotional wellbeing in a safe, judgment-free environment.

💬

Short-Term Counseling

Confidential sessions with licensed therapists focused on practical strategies and meaningful progress.

🚨

Crisis Intervention

Immediate, professional support during moments of acute distress, trauma, or personal crisis.

⚖️

Financial & Legal Coaching

Expert consultations to help navigate debt, legal concerns, estate planning, and life's practical challenges.

🌿

Work-Life Balance

Tools, guidance, and coaching to help manage competing demands and restore a sense of calm and purpose.

💙

Substance Abuse Support

Compassionate, non-judgmental assistance for those struggling with substance use — and support for their families.
